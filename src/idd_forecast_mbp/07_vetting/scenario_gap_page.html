What differs between the scenarios

In these models only two inputs take different values in different scenarios: GDP per capita (the SSP, from 2027 on) and malaria suitability (the RCP; it varies by draw). Model 7427 adds wind speed (RCP, by draw). Everything else that feeds the counts is the same in all three scenarios.

  • Same in every scenario: population (the count denominator and the age/sex split), DAH per capita, vaccine coverage and the vaccine factor per admin-2.
  • Different by scenario but not a model term: minimum temperature (from 2024) and flood exposure (from 2015).
  • Each admin-2 is shifted so its 2023 value equals the GBD-raked observation, so only the change in each input since 2023 moves the forecast.

Method and checks

    
For every admin-2, draw and year, the forecast's log rates are rebuilt from the fitted terms (each term exported on a 200,000-point grid from the saved model objects). With A0 the country effect and the shift fixing 2023 at the observation:

    
logit PfPR(t) = observed logit PfPR(2023) + Σ [term(input(t)) − term(input(2023))]
log rate(t)   = observed log rate(2023) + g(logit PfPR(t)) − g(observed logit PfPR(2023)) + γ [log GDP(t) − log GDP(2023)]

    
Any mix of inputs (some from scenario A, the rest from B, some held at 2023, some from the 2025 run) can then be evaluated with this run's model; that is what the split, the held counts and the factorial use. Counts are computed without vaccine protection and multiplied, per admin-2 and year, by the Baseline arm's vaccine factor (Baseline count / no-vaccine count), which is the same in all three scenarios.
